# Care to Learn amount history

The weekly maximum per child was £160 outside London and £175 for a young
parent whose home address was in London in the official [2014–15 EFA guide,
paragraph 3](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/government/uploads/system/uploads/attachment_data/file/310711/Care_to_Learn_guidance.pdf#page=4),
[2015–16 guide](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/care-to-learn-guide-for-institutions)
and [2022–23 ESFA guide](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/care-to-learn-guide-for-the-2022-to-2023-academic-year/care-to-learn-guide-for-the-2022-to-2023-academic-year).
The [2023–24 guide, section 1](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/care-to-learn-guide-for-the-2023-to-2024-academic-year/care-to-learn-guide-for-the-2023-to-2024-academic-year)
explicitly increases those amounts to £180 and £195 for that academic year.
The [2026–27 conditions, sections 2.2 and 4.1](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/care-to-learn-conditions-of-grant-funding/care-to-learn-academic-year-2026-to-2027-conditions-of-grant-funding)
retain the newer amounts. Intervening annual editions were not individually
verified, and this history does not establish pre-2014 rates.

The parameter dates `2015-01-01` and `2024-01-01` are annual model anchors.
They are not certified daily academic-year commencement dates or fiscal-year
averages. The model retains its existing 30 April parameter sample: annual
model years 2015–2023 use £160/£175, and 2024 onward uses £180/£195. The guides'
publication dates, examples of course start dates and summer-retainer deadlines
do not establish a universal day on which the rate changed. No academic-year
allocation or fiscal-year blending is introduced.

The amount formula still caps each qualifying child's annual childcare costs
at 52 times the weekly maximum and pays the benefit-unit total once. That is an
annual modelling approximation: the actual scheme funds eligible childcare
and travel based on attendance and costs. Travel, course dates and funded
weeks are not modelled. Existing eligibility proxies, including current age
rather than age when the course started, remain unchanged; this rate history
does not certify all course-funding, other-parent, immigration or other
eligibility conditions.
