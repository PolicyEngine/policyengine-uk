# Childcare Offer for Wales

The Childcare Offer for Wales gives eligible working parents, and parents in
further or higher education, of 3- and 4-year-olds up to 30 hours a week of
funded nursery education and childcare for 48 weeks a year. The Welsh
Government funds the childcare element at a national hourly rate; local
authorities fund the nursery education element.

The Offer runs on the Welsh Government's non-statutory guidance. Section 1
of the Childcare Funding (Wales) Act 2019 is not yet in force, and no
regulations have been made under it, so the parameters cite the guidance
and the Welsh Government's written statements.
