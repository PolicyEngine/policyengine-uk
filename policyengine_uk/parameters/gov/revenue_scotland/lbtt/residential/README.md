# Residential

## Additional Dwelling Supplement

[SSI 2024/367, articles 1–3](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ssi/2024/367/made)
raises the supplement from 6% to 8% for transactions with an effective date
on or after 5 December 2024. Transactions under contracts entered into on or
before 4 December 2024 retain the previous 6% rate, even if their effective
date is later. [Revenue Scotland's guidance](https://revenue.scot/taxes/land-buildings-transaction-tax/additional-dwelling-supplement-ads)
confirms the rate and transition.

The parameter retains the statutory 5 December 2024 change date. The model's
existing annual transactional-tax convention samples parameters on 30 April,
so annual 2024 calculations use 6% and annual 2025 onward calculations use
8%. The annual household purchase inputs have no contract or effective-date
field: they cannot select protected contracts or distinguish purchases before
and after the December change within 2024. These annual calculations do not
represent every actual transaction in those years. The rate update preserves
that convention and does not introduce a blended annual rate or new date
inputs.
