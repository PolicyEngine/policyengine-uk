# Owned company

Facts about a company in which a person stands in a position analogous to a
sole owner or partner (an owner-manager trading through a limited company, or
a controlling shareholder in a property company). Universal Credit treats such
a person as the sole owner or partner (Universal Credit Regulations 2013 reg.
77; Universal Credit Regulations (Northern Ireland) 2016 reg. 77); see
`gov/dwp/universal_credit/company/`.

Every input defaults to "no such company", so datasets that do not carry these
facts are unaffected.
