Added the 1 April 2023 National Minimum Wage and National Living Wage rates (S.I. 2023/354). Without them, 2023 used the April 2022 rates, for example £9.50 instead of £10.42 for workers aged 23 or over, which also understated the 2023 Universal Credit minimum income floor and the Tax-Free Childcare and extended childcare minimum income thresholds. Every minimum wage rate now cites the statutory instrument and regulation that set it, and a new test checks each age band from 2015 to 2026 against the statutory instruments.
