- Count other investment income (National Savings, securities and trust interest) in `total_income`, which income tax already charges, so post-tax income, Winter Fuel Payment and Housing Benefit/Council Tax Reduction non-dependant tests see it (#2174).
- Band the Housing Benefit non-dependant deduction by gross income only for a non-dependant in remunerative work (at least 16 hours a week); anyone else aged 18 or over takes the minimum deduction (HB Regs 2006 regs 6 and 74(1)-(2)). Make no deduction where the claimant or partner is blind or receives Attendance Allowance, the DLA care component, the PIP daily living component or Armed Forces Independence Payment (reg 74(6)).
