- Count only the claimant's and partner's income-related ESA and income-based JSA in Housing Benefit, council tax reduction and tax credit income, and in the passports for working-age council tax reduction, Scottish Child Payment and targeted childcare. Another member of the benefit unit, such as a non-dependent adult, who reports an award claims in their own right; their award still counts in household income.
- Exempt a council tax reduction non-dependant only through their own or their couple's Income Support, income-based JSA or income-related ESA, not another benefit-unit member's. The maintenance loan schedule for students entitled to benefits now reads the student's own or their couple's income-related ESA, alongside the existing parent and disability tests.
- Treat a family as claiming Income Support (`would_claim_IS`) when the claimant or partner reports it, or when every entitled benefit is claimed (`claims_all_entitled_benefits`); another member's report no longer counts.
