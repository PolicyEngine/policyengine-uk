- Benefit cap earnings exception (Universal Credit Regulations 2013 reg. 82) now measures each claimant's own earned income with `uc_individual_earned_income_before_mif`, rather than netting the claimant's whole income tax liability off the couple's combined earnings. Only income tax and National Insurance in respect of employment or self-employment are deducted (reg. 55(5)(b), reg. 57(2)), so tax on pension, property, savings and dividend income no longer removes the exception. Each person's earnings are floored at nil before the couple's are added, so one partner's pension contributions no longer reduce the other's earnings, and contributions paid after 75, which are not relievable (Finance Act 2004 s. 188(3)(a)), are no longer deducted.
- Moved the benefit cap earnings threshold out of three hardcoded copies of `10_152` into the dated parameter `gov.dwp.benefit_cap_earnings_exemption`, with values from 2020-21 to 2026-27 (2026-27 rises to £881 a month) derived from 16 hours a week at the National Living Wage.
- Armed Forces Independence Payment now excepts a benefit unit from the benefit cap, with each scheme's recipients: for a family on Universal Credit only a claimant's AFIP counts (UC Regs 2013 regs 2 and 83(1)(c)); for a family on Housing Benefit the claimant's, their partner's or a young person's (HB Regs 2006 reg 75F(1)(ea)). A child's never does. The variable has no dataset source, so this changes household calculations only.
- Removed dead copy-pasted code from the three `is_benefit_cap_exempt_*` variables, which computed exemption conditions that were never returned.
