- Moved the benefit cap earnings threshold (Universal Credit Regulations 2013 reg. 82) out of the hardcoded `10_152` into the dated monthly parameter `gov.dwp.benefit_cap_earnings_exemption`, with values from 2020-21 to 2026-27 derived from 16 hours a week at the National Living Wage. 2025-26 is unchanged at £846 a month; 2026-27 rises to £881.
- Armed Forces Independence Payment now lifts the benefit cap for each scheme's recipients: for a family with a Universal Credit award only a claimant's AFIP counts (UC Regs 2013 regs 2 and 83(1)(c)), so a dependent young person's no longer does; for a family on Housing Benefit the claimant's, their partner's or a young person's still counts (HB Regs 2006 reg 75F(1)(ea)).
- Added benefit cap exception regression tests, including the earnings exception's per-claimant earned income cases (tax on pension income, one partner's pension contributions, contributions after 75).
