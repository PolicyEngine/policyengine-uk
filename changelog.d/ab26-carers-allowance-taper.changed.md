Replace the cliff edge at the Carer's Allowance earnings limit with the 100% taper announced at Budget 2026, in England and Wales: the weekly allowance falls by £1 for each £1 of weekly earnings above the limit. The taper starts within 2030-31; until DWP confirms the date the model starts it on 6 October 2030, so 2030-31 carries 182 of its 365 days. In that year the weeks before the taper and the weeks under it each get their own couple restriction and overlapping-benefit reduction, so State Pension reduces the allowance only in the weeks it is paid, and a partner below the limit keeps the award until the other partner becomes eligible. Supplied or reformed amounts before the couple restriction or the overlap rule still reach the payment, and a payment of less than a penny for the year is nil.
