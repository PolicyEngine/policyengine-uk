Replace the cliff edge at the Carer's Allowance earnings limit with the 100% taper announced at Budget 2026, in England and Wales: the weekly allowance falls by £1 for each £1 of weekly earnings above the limit. The taper starts within 2030-31; until DWP confirms the date the model starts it on 6 October 2030, so 2030-31 carries 182 of its 365 days. In that year the weeks before the taper and the weeks under it each get their own couple restriction and overlapping-benefit reduction, so State Pension reduces the allowance only in the weeks the allowance pays for. A couple caring for the same person elect which of them has the allowance, and the model gives it to the partner with the larger award (before, it preferred the benefit-unit head), so the taper never leaves a couple with less than the cliff edge did, and one partner has the award for the year. An amount before the couple restriction or the overlap rule that an input supplies, or that a reform neutralizes, calculates with a formula of its own or otherwise changes, reaches the payment with the whole year's treatment for the stages after it, even where it equals the model's. The payment ignores the stage before the couple restriction wherever an input supplies, or a reform calculates, the amount before overlap. A mask that takes away a fraction of a penny counts as a change, since the couple restriction chooses only among partners with an award. A payment of less than half a penny for the year is nil.
