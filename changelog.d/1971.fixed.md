Pay the legacy enhanced disability premium for an award of enhanced PIP daily living or armed forces independence payment as well as the highest DLA care rate, and apply its State Pension Credit age limit. Count an award only where its amount is positive, and count an amount entered without its category when it reaches `52 × (top weekly rate − £1)`. Give four working-age Council Tax Reduction schemes their own lists. Scottish councils count attendance allowance at either rate from April 2023, and the Scottish Ministers removed the age limit from June 2022. Merton and Kingston upon Thames count either PIP daily living rate and omit armed forces independence payment; Newham counts either PIP daily living rate and armed forces independence payment. The model applies each council's list from the first scheme year a council text supports, and the Housing Benefit condition before then: Merton's from 2023-24, and Kingston upon Thames's and Newham's in every model year, since their texts support them from 2013-14.
