Universal Credit for a member of a couple who claims as a single person (UC Regs 2013 reg. 3(3)): single-claimant standard allowance at the claimant's own age, no LCWRA or carer element or benefit cap exception for the other member, the single-claimant benefit cap and the shared accommodation rate, while the couple's capital and income still count. A partner under 18 outside regulation 8 is now identified as such a partner.
