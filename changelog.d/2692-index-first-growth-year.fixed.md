Anchor ten growth indices (per-person GDP and mixed income, house prices, mortgage interest, petrol and diesel spending, rail journeys per person, population, the private pension index and lagged earnings) one year before their first listed growth year, so a direct input dated before that year is carried forward by that year's growth instead of a factor of 1. For example, a 2020 `rail_usage` input queried for 2021 grows by 2.5433. Dataset projection, which reads the growth rates, is unchanged, except that residential property values read the rounded house-price index, so their yearly ratios can move slightly (2021 to 2022: 1.0926 before, about 1.0925970 now).
