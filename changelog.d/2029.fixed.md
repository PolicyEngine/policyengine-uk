Let a non-dependant live with only some of a household's joint tenants in Housing Benefit and Council Tax Reduction (new input `non_dependant_normally_resides_with`; the default is every joint tenant, as before): only those joint tenants count the non-dependant in their Housing Benefit size criteria and shared accommodation test, including the severe disability premium conditions that test reads, and only they bear the non-dependant deduction in either benefit. Those Housing Benefit rules no longer treat a joint tenant with no rent of their own as a non-dependant.
