Let a non-dependant live with only some of a household's joint tenants in Housing Benefit and Council Tax Reduction (new input `non_dependant_normally_resides_with`; the default is every joint tenant, as before): only those joint tenants count the non-dependant in their size criteria and shared accommodation test, and only they bear the non-dependant deduction. Housing Benefit no longer treats a joint tenant with no rent of their own as a non-dependant.
