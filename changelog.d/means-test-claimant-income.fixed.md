- Universal Credit, Housing Benefit, Income Support, tax credits and Council Tax Reduction now assess the income of the claimant and any partner only, as Pension Credit already did. The earnings, pensions and savings income of a child, a young person or any other member of the benefit unit no longer reduce the award (UC Regulations 2013 reg 22; HB Regulations 2006 reg 25; IS Regulations 1987 reg 23; Tax Credits Act 2002 s.7; Council Tax Reduction Schemes (Prescribed Requirements) (England) Regulations 2012 Sch 1 para 11). The shared helper is `add_for_claimant_and_partner`.
