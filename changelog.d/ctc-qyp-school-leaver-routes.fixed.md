- Counted a 16-year-old who has left education as a Child Tax Credit qualifying young person until the 31 August after their 16th birthday, in any year that starts before it (Child Tax Credit Regulations 2002 reg. 4), and added an input for the 20 weeks after leaving while registered for work or training (reg. 5(3)(b)). Remunerative work of 24 hours a week ends both for a young person who has left education (reg. 5(4)(a)).
