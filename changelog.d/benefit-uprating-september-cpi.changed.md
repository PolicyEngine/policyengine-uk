Uprate benefits each April by the previous September's CPI, as the Social Security Administration Act 1992 s150 review does, instead of the previous calendar year's CPI. The personal allowance follows the same measure after its freeze (Income Tax Act 2007 s57). The Universal Credit standard allowance now applies the Universal Credit Act 2025 uplift (`rebalancing.standard_allowance_uplift`, previously unused) on top of September CPI from 2027-28, the LCWRA element is frozen to 2029-30, and the protected LCWRA amount reproduces the published £429.80 for 2026-27. Benefit rates that projected from an earlier start value (constant attendance allowance, disability premiums, Pension Credit additions) now project from their latest published rate, and Income Support, income-based JSA, IIDB, Pension Credit, tax credit and 2025-26 disability and carer rates now hold the published rates. Amounts outside the s150 review keep their previous projection.
