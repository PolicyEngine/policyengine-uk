Uprate benefits each April by the previous September's CPI, as the Social Security Administration Act 1992 s150 review does, instead of the previous calendar year's CPI. The personal allowance and the basic rate limit follow the same measure after their freeze, with the Income Tax Act 2007 s57 and s21 round-ups to £10 and £100. Universal Credit Act 2025 ss1-4: the standard allowance applies the Act's uplift (`rebalancing.standard_allowance_uplift`, previously unused) on top of September CPI from 2027-28; the LCWRA element is frozen to 2029-30 and then uprated by September CPI; the protected LCWRA amount for pre-2026 claimants follows s4 (one amount that keeps every standard allowance plus LCWRA in line with September CPI, £429.80 in 2026-27) and continues after 2029-30 instead of falling to the new-claimant rate. Benefit rates that projected from an earlier start value (constant attendance allowance, disability premiums, Pension Credit additions) now project from their latest published rate, and Income Support, income-based JSA, IIDB, Pension Credit, tax credit and 2025-26 disability and carer rates now hold the published rates. Amounts outside the s150 review (Pension Credit standard minimum guarantee and savings credit threshold, Housing Benefit pension-age allowances and earnings disregards, DfE funding rates, council tax high value surcharge) keep their previous projection.
