Uprate benefits each April by the previous September's CPI, the measure every Social Security Administration Act 1992 s150 review uses, instead of the previous calendar year's CPI (#2533). `gov.benefit_uprating_cpi` now follows `gov.economic_assumptions.indices.september_cpi_uprating`, so the Universal Credit standard allowance and health element indices built on it follow September CPI too, as the Universal Credit Act 2025 requires. Add September 2026 CPI (3.5%, ONS 21 October 2026), which sets the April 2027 rise. IIDB moves from same-year calendar CPI to the benefit index. Amounts outside the s150 prices review that were on the index (Pension Credit standard minimum guarantee and savings credit threshold, Housing Benefit pension-age allowances, DfE funding rates, council tax high value surcharge) keep their previous projection.
