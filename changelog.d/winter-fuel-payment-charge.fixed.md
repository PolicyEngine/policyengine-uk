- From the 2025 qualifying week, pay the Winter Fuel Payment to everyone of pensionable age in England, Wales and Northern Ireland (SI 2025/969 reg. 2; NISR 2025/142 reg. 2) and recover it through the winter fuel payment charge, in place of a household income test. The test paid a whole household if any member of pensionable age had total income under £35,000. It applied only in England and Wales, so pensioners in Northern Ireland not on a relevant benefit were paid nothing, and nothing stood in for the charge on the Pension Age Winter Heating Payment. Its boundary was also wrong: total income of exactly £35,000 is not charged.
