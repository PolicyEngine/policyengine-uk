- From the 2025 qualifying week, pay the Winter Fuel Payment to everyone of pensionable age in England, Wales and Northern Ireland (SI 2025/969 reg. 2; NISR 2025/142 reg. 2) and recover it through the winter fuel payment charge, in place of a household income test. The test paid a whole household if any member of pensionable age had total income under £35,000. It applied only in England and Wales, and it charged nobody in Northern Ireland or on the Pension Age Winter Heating Payment. Its boundary was also wrong: total income of exactly £35,000 is not charged.
- Count the Pension Age Winter Heating Payment in `household_benefits`, `hbai_household_net_income` and `hbai_benefits`, as the Winter Fuel Payment is.
