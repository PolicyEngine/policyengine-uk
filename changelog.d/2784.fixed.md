PolicyEngine now counts the claimant's and partner's Maternity Allowance in the benefit cap, as Parliament lists it among the welfare benefits regulations may cap (Welfare Reform Act 2012 s.96(10)(i)). DWP and councils now take the allowance off a capped family's Universal Credit or Housing Benefit, as they do the family's other capped benefits, and still leave a non-dependent adult's allowance out (#2784).
