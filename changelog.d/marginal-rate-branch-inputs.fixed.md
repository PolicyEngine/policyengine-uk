`marginal_tax_rate`, `marginal_tax_rate_wrt_employer_cost` and `marginal_tax_rate_on_capital_gains` now recalculate, in their branches, every variable not entered for the year. They used to keep any variable stored at load for any year, so past the data years a calculated value such as `student_loan_repayment` was read from before the pay rise when it had been calculated before the branch was created, and fresh single-year runs left the 9% student loan repayment out of the rate.
