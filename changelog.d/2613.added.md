Model the exceptions to the Universal Credit and Child Tax Credit two-child limit (UC Regulations 2013 Schedule 12 and CTC Regulations 2002 regs 9 to 14): multiple births, adoption, non-parental caring arrangements, non-consensual conception and continuation of an earlier exception, as person-level inputs that default to false. Adopted children and children in non-parental care are left out of the Universal Credit order of children, as reg 24B(2A) requires.
