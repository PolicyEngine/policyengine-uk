Charge non-dependant deductions in the English working-age local Council Tax Reduction schemes (Merton, Kingston upon Thames, Newham, Westminster, Oxford) as the national schemes do: for adults in the applicant's own benefit unit (the applicant identified as for the means test, so a grandmother who applies alone is charged for the couple in her benefit unit), separately for each adult in another family who is not its claimant or partner, and apportioned between jointly liable applicants. Band a claimant or partner on the couple's joint income and anyone else on their own, each on their own hours; exempt a non-dependant on Income Support, income-based JSA, income-related ESA or State Pension Credit only if the award is payable to them, and one on Universal Credit only if they have no earned income of their own; and deduct for each member of a Universal Credit couple in Westminster, as the Default Scheme does. In every scheme, national and local, someone who pays the householder for their own board or lodging, or is a joint tenant, is not a non-dependant even where the rest of their family is, except a member of the household head's own family who pays board, who is taken to be a close relative.
