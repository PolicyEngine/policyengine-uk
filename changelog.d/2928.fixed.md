The model now takes the rent, less any charge for meals, as the Housing Benefit eligible rent of a private renter in a houseboat, caravan or mobile home, instead of capping it at the Local Housing Allowance (SI 2006/213 and SI 2006/214 reg 13C(5)(d)(i); SR 2006/405 and SR 2006/406 reg 14C(5)(d)(i)). `LHA_eligible` returns false where `accommodation_type` is `MOBILE`, and `housing_benefit_eligible` reads `benunit_is_renting`, so these families stay eligible and a supplied `LHA_eligible` no longer decides eligibility. The model has no rent officer determination, so it can overstate their Housing Benefit.
