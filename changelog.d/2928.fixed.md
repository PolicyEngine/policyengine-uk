Housing Benefit no longer caps the eligible rent of a private renter in a houseboat, caravan or mobile home at the Local Housing Allowance (SI 2006/213 and SI 2006/214 reg 13C(5)(d)(i); SR 2006/405 and SR 2006/406 reg 14C(5)(d)(i)). `LHA_eligible` is false where `accommodation_type` is `MOBILE`, and `housing_benefit_eligible` tests renting directly, so these families keep Housing Benefit.
