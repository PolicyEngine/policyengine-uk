Fixed the Carer's Allowance earnings limit treating earnings a penny a week over the 2020-21 to 2024-25 limits (£128, £132, £139 and £151) as within them, and the Budget 2026 earnings taper taking slightly less than the pence above the limit off the award. Net earnings for Carer's Allowance and the Scottish Carer Support Payment now apply the Computation of Earnings Regulations' rounding to each step in exact pence, and the formula gives the weekly excess over the Carer's Allowance limit in whole pence for a limit in whole pence.
