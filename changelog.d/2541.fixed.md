Fixed the Carer's Allowance earnings limit treating earnings a penny a week over the 2020-21 to 2024-25 limits (£128, £132, £139 and £151) as within them. Net earnings now apply the Computation of Earnings Regulations' rounding to each step: pay and profit are taken down to a whole penny and each calculated deduction (notional tax, Class 4 and half of pension contributions) up to one; the model's PAYE and Class 1, which in law are the amounts actually deducted, are rounded up too. Earnings are worked in double precision from the whole pence each amount was entered as, and a sum the model forms (pay, pension contributions, NI income) is the exact sum of its parts. Class 1 is worked out again from its formula where the model calculated it with that formula; entered as an input (for the year or month by month), or calculated under a reform, it is deducted as entered or calculated, its months summed exactly, so £1.20 entered is £1.20 deducted. The limit test reads the whole pence back exactly, so float32 rounding no longer decides it. Pension contributions entered as a total still reduce earnings. The Scottish Carer Support Payment, which reads the same earnings, is judged exactly at its limit too.
