Fixed the Carer's Allowance earnings limit treating earnings a penny a week over the 2020-21 to 2024-25 limits (£128, £132, £139 and £151) as within them. Net earnings now apply the Computation of Earnings Regulations' rounding to each step: pay and profit are taken down to a whole penny and each deduction (tax, National Insurance and half of pension contributions) up to one. They are worked in double precision from the whole pence each amount was entered as, and the limit test reads the whole pence back exactly, so float32 rounding no longer decides it.
