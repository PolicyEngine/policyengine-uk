A bare-year reform key on the economic growth rates, the indices built from them and the household parameters now names that calendar year. It used to name the fiscal year from 6 April, which moved the next year: a 2027 earnings growth rate showed as 2027's but moved the 2028 index and the April 2029 triple lock. Reforms to series built during processing (the triple lock rate, the indices, the lagged series) now uprate what is built from them, and deprecated `labor_supply_responses` paths in reform dicts reach the parameters formulas read.
