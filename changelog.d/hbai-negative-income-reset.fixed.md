- HBAI household net income before housing costs now resets a negative figure to zero, as HBAI does; income after housing costs, deciles, poverty flags and poverty gaps follow from the reset figure. Poverty headcounts do not change while the median person's income is positive (as on the enhanced FRS); poverty gaps fall and income totals rise by the sum of the negative incomes. Households whose income is reset now count in the bottom income decile, while households at zero hold at most a tenth of people, instead of being left out with decile -1, so bottom-decile figures move. The figure before the reset is the new `hbai_household_net_income_before_reset_to_zero`, which the labour supply responses use, so they are unchanged.
