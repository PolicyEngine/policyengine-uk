Work out the winter fuel payment charge in the property allowance election only where it can change the choice. Add `winter_fuel_payment_charge_ceiling`, the most the charge could be whatever benefits are received, and settle each election on income tax before the charge wherever the ceilings on the two routes cannot reverse it. Each household's landlords now decide in their own order, and every household's next decision is compared in one pair of branches, so the elections that turn on the charge are compared on income tax including it together rather than once per round and rank. Routes, income tax and household net income are unchanged; a Microcosm UK 2024-25 microsimulation builds and calculates 2026 household net income in about half the time.
