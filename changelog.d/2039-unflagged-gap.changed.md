- Without any parent flag, a member at least 20 years younger than the claimant is now presumed to be their child at any age (`presumed_child.minimum_age_gap_at_any_age`), so ages 50 and 25 entered without relationships are a single claimant and an adult child, not a couple. Members 16 to 19 years younger stay partners from age 20. The presumption only applies where `is_claimant_or_partner` is not supplied: a dataset that carries it (such as policyengine-uk-data's enhanced FRS once it supplies the FRS roles) is unaffected, while datasets without it are not.
