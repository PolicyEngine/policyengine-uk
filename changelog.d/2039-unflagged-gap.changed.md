- Without any parent flag, a member at least 20 years younger than the claimant is now presumed to be their child at any age (`presumed_child.minimum_age_gap_at_any_age`), so ages 50 and 25 entered without relationships are a single claimant and an adult child, not a couple. Members 16 to 19 years younger stay partners from age 20. Survey datasets supply `is_claimant_or_partner`, so the presumption only applies to inputs without it.
