Round projected State Pension weekly rates as each Up-rating Order does. Each April's full new State Pension and basic State Pension rate is now the previous year's rate raised by the triple lock and rounded to the nearest 5p (`gov.dwp.state_pension.weekly_rate_rounding`), instead of the last published rate times the index's growth, unrounded. On the October 2026 forecast the full new State Pension is £250.70, £259.20 and £265.70 a week in 2027-28 to 2029-30 (was £250.71, £259.24 and £265.72), and 52 weeks of the 2027-28 rate is the £13,036.40 of HM Treasury's Pension Tax Allowance explainer. The triple lock index is no longer rounded to five decimal places.
