Tested that Pension Credit, Child Benefit and Child Tax Credit each stop counting a young person who receives Income Support in their own right as a qualifying young person, and Universal Credit does not (SPC Regs 2002 reg. 4A(5); Child Benefit (General) Regulations 2006 reg. 8(2)(a); CTC Regs 2002 reg. 5(4)(c); UC Regs 2013 reg. 5(5)), in the baseline and under reforms that remove Income Support. Pension Credit and Child Tax Credit read the Income Support the model calculates for a claimant or partner, so a reform that pays no Income Support ends the exclusion for a claimant or partner; Child Benefit reads the young person's own report while Income Support is in payment.
