Stopped counting a young person who receives Income Support in their own right as a qualifying young person for Pension Credit, Child Benefit (and so Housing Benefit and the legacy benefits) and Child Tax Credit (SPC Regs 2002 reg. 4A(5); Child Benefit (General) Regulations 2006 reg. 8(2)(a); CTC Regs 2002 reg. 5(4)(c)). Pension Credit and Child Tax Credit read the Income Support the model calculates for a claimant or partner, so a reform that pays no Income Support ends their exclusion; Child Benefit reads the young person's own report while Income Support is in payment.
