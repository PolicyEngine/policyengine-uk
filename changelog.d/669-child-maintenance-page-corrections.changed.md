Corrected the child maintenance planning page (`docs/book/programs/gov/cms/child-maintenance.md`): the Child Maintenance Service default rate for one child is £39 (SI 2012/2677 reg. 49), shared-care fractions are averaged across the children in each carer's care and the further £7 reduction for 175 or more nights is per child with a £7 floor, UC reg. 66(1)(d) covers maintenance from a current as well as a former spouse or civil partner, and the model reproduces UC's treatment of maintenance but not the paying parent's combined marginal rates described by the Centre for Social Justice.
