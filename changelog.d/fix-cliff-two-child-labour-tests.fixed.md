- `cliff_evaluated` no longer flags people under 18: it now covers exactly the adults whose marginal tax rate is simulated (`adult_index` 1 to `gov.simulation.marginal_tax_rate_adults`).
- The Universal Credit two-child limit age exemption reform (`gov.contrib.two_child_limit.age_exemption.universal_credit`) now lifts the two-child limit for exempt families, so their third and later children get the child element. It no longer changes who gets the higher first-child amount, which still requires a first child born before April 2017. Its tests no longer pay the parent a child element.
- Removed `tests/behavioral_responses/test_labour_supply_responses.yaml`, which `make test` never ran and which failed under `policyengine-core test`. Its cases now run in `test_behavioral_responses.py` without survey data, alongside a check that a real tax change produces labour supply responses.
