Give each member of the benefit unit aged 16 or over who is not the claimant or partner a bedroom in the Universal Credit LHA size criteria, as a qualifying young person the renter is responsible for (UC Regs 2013 Sch 4 para 10(1)(b)) or as a non-dependant (para 10(1)(c)). A qualifying young person no one is responsible for, such as one looked after by a local authority, adds no bedroom in any family (para 9(2)(g)), and nor does a member who heads the household, does not claim and is not a qualifying young person the renter is responsible for, because the formula treats the household head as liable for the rent and so not a non-dependant (para 9(2)(d)).
