Give each member of the benefit unit aged 16 or over who is not the claimant, partner or household head a bedroom in the Universal Credit LHA size criteria, as a qualifying young person (UC Regs 2013 Sch 4 para 10(1)(b)) or as a non-dependant (para 10(1)(c)). A qualifying young person no one is responsible for, such as one looked after by a local authority, adds no bedroom in any family (para 9(2)(g)), and nor does a household head who does not claim, whom the model treats as liable for the rent (para 9(2)(d)).
