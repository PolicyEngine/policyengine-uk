The model applies the 2020–21 residential SDLT relief dates that Parliament set in the SDLT (Temporary Relief) Act 2020 and Finance Act 2021 s87. It runs a £500,000 nil band without first-time buyers' relief to 30 June 2021, then a £250,000 nil band from 1 July to 30 September 2021. In model year 2021 it charges no SDLT on a main-home purchase up to £500,000. The model also dates the residential lease rent table to those periods and adds its £250,000 nil band from 23 September 2022 to 31 March 2025.
