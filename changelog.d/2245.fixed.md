Stamp Duty Land Tax now runs the £500,000 nil band, without first-time buyers' relief, to 30 June 2021 and a £250,000 nil band from 1 July to 30 September 2021, as the SDLT (Temporary Relief) Act 2020 and Finance Act 2021 s87 set, so 2021 simulations use the relief. The residential lease rent table gains the same dates and the 2022-25 £250,000 nil band.
