Disregard the whole income and capital of Pension Credit guarantee credit recipients in the England pensioner, Wales and Scotland council tax reduction schemes, so they get the maximum reduction less non-dependant deductions whatever their income or savings, and use the Pension Credit assessment of income (plus the savings credit) and capital for savings-credit-only recipients (SI 2012/2885 Sch 1 paras 13-14; WSI 2013/3029 Sch 1 paras 7-8; SSI 2012/319 regs 24-25).
