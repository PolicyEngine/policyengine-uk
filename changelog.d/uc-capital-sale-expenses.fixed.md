Value Universal Credit, Housing Benefit, Income Support, income-based JSA, income-related ESA and Pension Credit capital at market value less 10% where a sale would incur expenses and less any debt secured on the asset, as UC Regs 2013 reg. 49(1) and its legacy equivalents require. The 10% applies to land, property, directly held shares and all of a `corporate_wealth` that the dataset does not itemise, not to cash savings. Unitemised `corporate_wealth` can still fold in unit trusts, ISAs and, on datasets built before private pension wealth was split out, non-defined-benefit pension wealth. Adds secured-debt inputs for other residential property, non-residential property and land, and the council tax reduction formula deducts the debt secured on land from the land on its own household route. Each programme rounds a fraction of a penny in its capital calculations as its regulations require: to the nearest penny for Universal Credit (UC Regs 2013 reg. 6(1)) and Housing Benefit, and down for the legacy benefits and Pension Credit. The model works out a Lifetime ISA's surrender value from its balance and rounds it under the same rule, and keeps explicit Lifetime ISA capital inputs and reformed valuation formulas.
