Value Universal Credit, Housing Benefit, Income Support, income-based JSA, income-related ESA and Pension Credit capital at market value less 10% where a sale would incur expenses and less any debt secured on the asset, as UC Regs 2013 reg. 49(1) and its legacy equivalents require. The 10% applies to land, property and share-like capital (share_like_capital), not to cash savings; share-like capital also holds unit trusts, which have no costs of sale, and in datasets built before the private pension split it holds pension wealth, so the deduction reaches those too until the data separate them. Adds secured-debt inputs for other residential property, non-residential property and land. Each programme rounds a fraction of a penny in its capital calculations as its regulations require: to the nearest penny for Universal Credit (UC Regs 2013 reg. 6(1)) and Housing Benefit, and down for the legacy benefits and Pension Credit. A Lifetime ISA's surrender value is worked out from its balance and rounded under the same rule.
