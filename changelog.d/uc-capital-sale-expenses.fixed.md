Value Universal Credit, Housing Benefit, Income Support, income-based JSA, income-related ESA and Pension Credit capital at market value less 10% where a sale would incur expenses and less any debt secured on the asset, as UC Regs 2013 reg. 49(1) and its legacy equivalents require. The 10% applies to land, property and corporate_wealth, not to cash savings; corporate_wealth also holds unit trusts, which have no costs of sale, and in datasets built before the private pension split it holds pension wealth, so the deduction reaches those too until the data separate them. Adds secured-debt inputs for other residential property, non-residential property and land.
