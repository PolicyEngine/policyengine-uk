The model now counts a carer's Universal Credit award with the carer element when deciding the severe disability premium and the Pension Credit severe disability addition. In Scotland's working-age council tax reduction, it counts such awards for the carer premium from 1 April 2022. Until 31 March 2025 it also counts an award that DWP tapers to nil because of the carer's earnings, under its reading of Scotland's definition of an award "with a value of zero" (#2120, #2124).
