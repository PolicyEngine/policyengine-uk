The model now counts a carer's Universal Credit award with the carer element when deciding the severe disability premium and the Pension Credit severe disability addition. In Scotland's working-age council tax reduction, it counts such awards for the carer premium from 1 April 2022. Until 31 March 2025 it also counts a nil award that earnings bring about, reading Scotland's definition of an award "with a value of zero" to include one (#2120, #2124).
