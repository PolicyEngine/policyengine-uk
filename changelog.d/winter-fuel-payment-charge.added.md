- Add the winter fuel payment charge (`winter_fuel_payment_charge`, ITEPA 2003 s.681I, from 2025-26) with parameters under `gov.hmrc.income_tax.charges.winter_fuel_payment`. It is charged person by person: the whole Winter Fuel Payment (England, Wales and Northern Ireland) or Pension Age Winter Heating Payment, when the person's own total income exceeds £35,000 and they are not entitled to Income Support, income-based JSA, Pension Credit, income-related ESA or Universal Credit. It is added to `income_tax` after tax reductions (ITA 2007 s.23 Step 7).
- Add `income_tax_before_winter_fuel_payment_charge`. The Pension Credit, Income Support, Housing Benefit, Council Tax Reduction, Universal Credit and benefit cap means tests now deduct it instead of `income_tax` (Universal Credit deducted `tax`, income tax plus National Insurance). The charge recovers a payment rather than taxing the income those tests assess, and it depends on Pension Credit and Universal Credit receipt, so deducting it would make the model circular.
