Compute the State Pension triple lock from its statutory inputs, September CPI and May-July average weekly earnings total pay growth, instead of calendar-year OBR growth. The inputs hold the published figures each uprating review used, then forecasts: the OBR's September CPI forecast and its Q2 earnings growth (a proxy for May-July AWE) to the end of the EFO, and calendar-year growth after. Macro scenarios that edit calendar-year growth move them. April 2027 now rises by 3.9% (published May-July 2026 earnings) rather than 3.4%, and the rates run to the end of the economic-assumption series instead of 2034. The `active` toggle, which had no effect, now leaves the statutory minimum of earnings growth.
