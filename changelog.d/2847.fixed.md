The Cost of Living Payments now read zero before 2022, so the model no longer pays them in 2015-16 to 2021-22. As under the Social Security (Additional Payments) Acts 2022 and 2023, the model pays the means-tested payment to each benefit unit on Universal Credit, Pension Credit, income-based JSA, income-related ESA or Income Support, or on tax credits alone (at least £26 a year in 2022-23), and not on Housing Benefit alone, and pays the £150 disability payment to each person on a qualifying disability benefit.
