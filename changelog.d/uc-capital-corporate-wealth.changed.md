Universal Credit capital test: excluded `corporate_wealth` from the countable capital sources as an interim approximation pending a dataset-side pension split (policyengine-uk-data#452). The dataset builds that variable as private pension wealth (which Schedule 10 paragraph 10 of the Universal Credit Regulations 2013 disregards) bundled with shares, investment ISAs and collective investments (which the law counts); counting the bundle failed the capital test for claimants whose only large asset is a pension, and excluding it leaves the share-like components uncounted until the dataset separates them. Housing Benefit, JSA, ESA, Income Support and Pension Credit still count the bundle through their own source lists, and the CI regression fixture for the UC taper reform was re-baselined to the new caseload.
