Fix `calculate_dependency_contributions` for monthly variables. It raised "Inconsistent input" whenever the target or a variable it reads directly is defined monthly (Class 1 NI, child benefit amounts, fuel duty, energy price guarantee), because zeroing or restoring a monthly value over a year clashed with the months already stored. Each dependency is now zeroed on a throwaway branch, so the simulation passed in is left unchanged, and a monthly target's dependencies are what its months read rather than the target itself.
