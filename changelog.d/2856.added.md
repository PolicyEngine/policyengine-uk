Add `soc2020_major_group` and `soc2020_sub_major_group`, person inputs for the SOC 2020 major group (1 to 9) and sub-major group (a two-digit code whose first digit is the major group) of the person's main job, or of their last job if they are not in work, with 0 for none or unknown, so that datasets can carry occupation. The FRS codes the major group; the sub-major group has to be imputed within it from a survey that codes it.
