- Pay the Pension Credit severe disability addition only as SPC Regs 2002 Sch I para 1 allows: to a couple only where both partners receive a qualifying benefit or one does and the other is certified blind, at the reg 6(5)(b) double rate only without para 1(2)(b) hospital deeming or a carer benefit for either partner, and never where an adult aged 18 or over not ignored by Sch I para 2 lives in the household. A claimant's own carer benefit no longer bars their own addition, and the 2023-24 to 2025-26 statutory amounts are added.
- Apply reg 4A's qualifying young person dates to Pension Credit: every 16-year-old until the 1 September after their 16th birthday, and education or training only until the 1 September after the 19th birthday.
- Test the Savings Credit age condition (SPCA 2002 s.3(1)(a)) on attaining pensionable age before 6 April 2016 and age 65, from the person's birth cohort, instead of a calendar-year comparison against the State Pension age in force in the modelled year.
- Apply the SPCA 2002 s.4(1A) mixed-age couple exclusion only from 15 May 2019.
