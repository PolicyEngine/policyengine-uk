Give the Universal Credit LCWRA element, carer element and work allowance on the claimants' circumstances only (UC Regs 2013 regs 27(1), 29(1) and 22(1)(b)): a disabled child gave the claimant the LCWRA element on top of the disabled child addition, a child or qualifying young person who was a carer gave the claimant the carer element, and a dependant's limited capability gave a work allowance. Joint claimants who both have limited capability now have one LCWRA element (reg. 27(4)), each joint claimant who is a carer has a carer element, and a carer who has the LCWRA element does not also have the carer element (reg. 29(2) and (4)). The disability basic income contrib reform's LCWRA route follows: only a claimant with limited capability qualifies.
