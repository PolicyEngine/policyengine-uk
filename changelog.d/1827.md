- Rank `calculate_earnings_quintile` over adults on potential earnings rather than over the whole population on actual earnings. Ranking everyone, children included, left the bottom two quintiles entirely without earners, placed every potential labour-market entrant at the steep end of the OBR Table A1 elasticities, and left those quintiles with no employed donors — so `impute_wages_for_nonworkers` returned a wage of zero for them, which silently bars entry into employment. Wage donors are now grouped by sex and age band, which also breaks the circular dependency between the two functions.
