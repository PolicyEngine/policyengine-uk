- Move working-age families onto Universal Credit when a legacy benefit they receive closes (tax credits from 2025-26, Income Support and income-based JSA from 2026-27). A new dataset input, `would_claim_uc_at_legacy_closure` (default true), says whether the family claims; `claims_universal_credit` replaces `would_claim_uc` wherever the model asks whether a family claims Universal Credit; and the family's other legacy awards (Housing Benefit, Income Support, income-related ESA, income-based JSA) end at the closure whether or not it claims (SI 2014/1230 regs 8 and 46).
