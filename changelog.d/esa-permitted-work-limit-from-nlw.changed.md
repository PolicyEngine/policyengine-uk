The ESA permitted work higher earnings limit is now calculated as 16 x the national living wage, rounded up to the next 50p (ESA Regs 2008 reg 45(4) and (9A)), so it follows a change to gov.hmrc.minimum_wage instead of being listed year by year and projected with average earnings. The gov.dwp.ESA.exempt_work.higher_earnings_limit parameter is removed, and the minimum wage rates now start at 1 October 2010.
