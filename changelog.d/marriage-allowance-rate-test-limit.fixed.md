The search for the least-tax share of reliefs and allowances now applies the Marriage Allowance rate test at the statutory limits, instead of with room below them (about 3.9p near the £37,700 basic rate limit), so a recipient whose only qualifying share leaves income at a limit gets the reduction. The search keeps the room where float32 could hold income entered over a limit at the limit: where an amount the test reads reaches £65,536, or where the model cannot bound the float32 error in the amounts behind it, such as pay before expenses, under the least amount the entries can sit over a limit.
