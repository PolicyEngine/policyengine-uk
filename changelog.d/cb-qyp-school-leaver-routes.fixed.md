- Added the Child Benefit qualifying young person conditions for young people who have left education (Child Benefit (General) Regulations 2006 regs 4, 5 and 7): a 16-year-old counts until the 31 August after their 16th birthday, in any year that starts before it, and two new inputs place the 20-week extension period and the terminal date. Housing Benefit, the legacy benefits and Council Tax Reduction young persons follow. Child Benefit is not paid for a young person who qualifies only through the terminal date and works 24 hours or more a week (reg. 7(3)).
