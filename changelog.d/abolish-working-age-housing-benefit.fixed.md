Abolish working-age Housing Benefit awards outside specified and temporary accommodation from 1 July 2026 in Great Britain (SI 2025/1148 art. 7, inserted by SI 2026/409) and 1 October 2026 in Northern Ireland (SR 2025/176 art. 7, inserted by SR 2026/115). The new `gov.dwp.housing_benefit.working_age_awards_payable` parameter is day-weighted across each fiscal year, so 2026-27 pays an unsaved continuing award whose family does not claim Universal Credit for 86/365 of the year in Great Britain and 178/365 in Northern Ireland, and from 2027-28 there is none. Families meeting the Pension Credit age conditions keep their awards: a single claimant or both members of a couple over the qualifying age, and mixed-age couples with the SI 2019/37 art. 4 saving (SI 2014/1230 reg 6A(4)-(5)), and so do families whose Housing Benefit claimant personally holds an income-related ESA award that the appointee saving keeps (art. 7(4)(b), `esa_income_appointee_saving`): the model takes the only member reporting Housing Benefit, or a sole claimant where no member reports it, as the Housing Benefit claimant, counts the saved award only on a claimant or partner, and lets `housing_benefit_claimant_is_saved_esa_claimant` confirm the identity instead, overriding the reports. A family whose award is abolished claims Universal Credit if `would_claim_uc_at_legacy_closure` is true, which ends the claimant's and partner's income-related ESA.
