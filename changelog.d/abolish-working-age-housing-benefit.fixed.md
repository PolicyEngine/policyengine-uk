- Abolish working-age Housing Benefit awards outside specified and temporary accommodation from 1 July 2026 in Great Britain (SI 2025/1148 art. 7, inserted by SI 2026/409) and 1 October 2026 in Northern Ireland (SR 2025/176 art. 7, inserted by SR 2026/115). The new `gov.dwp.housing_benefit.working_age_awards_payable` parameter is day-weighted across each fiscal year, so 2026-27 pays a working-age continuing award for 86/365 of the year in Great Britain and 178/365 in Northern Ireland, and from 2027-28 there is none. Families with a member over State Pension age keep their awards (the pension-age and mixed-age savings).
