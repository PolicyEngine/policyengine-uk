A dataset that stores a household `cash_isa`, `stocks_and_shares_isa` or `household_lifetime_isa_balance` total keeps it after the last year it holds, those after 2032 included, grown by the index of the person balances it stands for, unless the dataset holds those balances for a later year. An input frame or a multi-year dataset that stores a total now grows it the same way, rather than summing person balances it does not hold. Means-tested capital no longer drops from 2033 by the cash ISA holdings, or by stocks-and-shares ISA holdings that `corporate_wealth` does not already cover.
