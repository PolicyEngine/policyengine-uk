The model now calculates Housing Benefit and national Council Tax Reduction non-dependant deductions under each scheme's rules. Bands include their lower edge. For a non-exempt non-dependant outside remunerative work, including someone on Income Support, income-based JSA or income-related ESA, the council deducts the lowest amount whatever their income. The council bands a couple on its joint gross income plus the family's awards, counting Universal Credit after the benefit cap and before debt deductions, and normally makes one deduction, the higher. Under Wales's working-age scheme, it deducts both members' amounts when their own Universal Credit award assesses either member. It deducts separately for each other adult. The model counts other investment income, ISA interest and ISA dividends in gross income, and treats a self-employment or property loss as nil. Councils apply the claimant and non-dependant exemptions, and for the Universal Credit exemption the model reads Universal Credit's own calculation of earned income. The model gives the England pensioner, Scottish and Welsh Council Tax Reduction schemes their own scales from 2013-14, and follows each jointly liable applicant's own scheme. For the Universal Credit benefit cap, the model now totals welfare benefits without Housing Benefit (`uc_benefit_cap_welfare_benefits`).
