The model now makes Housing Benefit and national Council Tax Reduction non-dependant deductions under each scheme's rules. Bands include their lower edge. For a non-dependant outside remunerative work, or on Income Support, income-based JSA or income-related ESA, the council deducts the lowest amount whatever their income. It makes one deduction for a couple, banded on the couple's gross income plus the family's awards (Universal Credit after the benefit cap, tax credits, child benefit and the income-related benefits), and a separate one for any other adult in the family. Gross income counts other investment income, ISA interest and ISA dividends, and treats a self-employment or property loss as nil. The claimant and non-dependant exemptions apply, and the Universal Credit exemption reads Universal Credit's own calculation of earned income. The England pensioner, Scottish and Welsh Council Tax Reduction schemes use their own scales from 2013-14, and each jointly liable applicant's deduction follows its own scheme.
