- Apply Housing Benefit and national Council Tax Reduction non-dependant deductions as legislated. Bands include their lower edge. A non-dependant outside remunerative work, or on Income Support, income-based JSA or income-related ESA, pays the lowest amount whatever their income. A couple pays one deduction, banded on the couple's gross income plus the family's awards (Universal Credit after the benefit cap, tax credits, child benefit and the income-related benefits), and any other adult in the family pays their own. Gross income counts other investment income and treats a self-employment or property loss as nil. The claimant and non-dependant exemptions apply. The England pensioner, Scottish and Welsh Council Tax Reduction schemes use their own scales from 2013-14, and each jointly liable applicant's deduction follows its own scheme.
