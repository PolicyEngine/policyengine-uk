Model Marriage Allowance as ITA 2007 ss. 55A-55E provide. The electing spouse gives up the fixed transferable amount (10% of the personal allowance, rounded up to £10) from their own allowance, and the other spouse's tax falls at Step 6 by the basic rate (or Scottish basic rate) times that amount, capped at their tax. Previously the recipient's taxable income fell by the transferor's unused allowance. The couple elects only when the conditions in s. 55B(2) and s. 55C(1) are met and it lowers their combined income tax. Universal Credit deducts the reduction from the recipient's tax on their earnings, so a pensioner's State Pension no longer changes the earning partner's earned income.
