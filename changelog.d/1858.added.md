- Add three person-level inputs, `capital_gains_badr`, `capital_gains_residential_property` and `capital_gains_carried_interest`, as components of `capital_gains`, and charge each at its own schedule in `capital_gains_tax`: new parameters `gov.hmrc.cgt.badr.rate` and `.lifetime_limit`, `gov.hmrc.cgt.residential_property.{basic,higher,additional}_rate` and `gov.hmrc.cgt.carried_interest.{basic,higher,additional}_rate`. The annual exempt amount goes to the highest-rate schedule first, relief gains take the unused basic rate band before other gains (TCGA 1992 s. 1I(4)-(6)), and the remaining band goes where it saves most. With the inputs absent or zero the liability is unchanged.
