The Universal Credit education protection for a claimant moved off legacy benefits (SI 2014/1230 reg. 60(1); SR 2016/226 reg. 61(1) in Northern Ireland) now needs a full-time course that the Universal Credit Regulations 2013 reg. 12(2) covers: advanced education, or a course with a student loan or grant for maintenance. A young person on another full-time course met the condition on the migration day, so the protection doesn't reach them, and the model no longer pays them Universal Credit through it.
