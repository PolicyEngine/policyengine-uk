- Read "paid to" and "entitled through the couple" separately for the winter heating payments, now that the legacy award readers name the one member of a couple an award is paid to. Under SI 2000/729, SI 2025/969 and NISR 2025/142 a person is on a relevant benefit only when it is paid to them (Universal Credit and tax credits are joint awards). Under SI 2024/869 reg 2(5), NISR 2024/160 reg 2(5) and SSI 2024/351 (reg 7(2), 10(8) and 2A) a member of a couple counts as on one when the other member is (`partner_receipt_counts`). A couple on a relevant benefit still receives one payment. `is_on_pension_credit` names the member Pension Credit is paid to, as the other readers do.
