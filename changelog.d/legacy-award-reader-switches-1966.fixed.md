The Childcare Offer for Wales income test now counts only the claimant's and partner's income-related ESA and income-based JSA towards a parent's gross income, not an award that another member of the benefit unit claims in their own right. New tests check that only the applicant's or partner's own income-related ESA gives a single applicant under 25 the higher council tax reduction personal allowance in Scotland and Wales. New tests check that the Housing Benefit and council tax reduction non-dependant exemptions for Income Support, income-based JSA and income-related ESA go only to the person the award is payable to, and that another member's award leaves a non-dependant couple banded on its own income.
