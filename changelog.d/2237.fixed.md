Relieve pension contributions up to the annual limit in Finance Act 2004 s. 190 (relevant UK earnings, raised to £3,600 where lower, the increase only through relief at source, s. 191(7)) instead of the annual allowance, and charge the annual allowance charge once on the excess (s. 227), so contributions above the allowance are no longer taxed twice. The charge now taxes the chargeable amount as the top slice of reduced net income at the basic, higher and additional rates (s. 227(4A)), with the rate limits raised by relief-at-source contributions and grossed-up Gift Aid (s. 227(4C)), or at the Scottish rates with the starter band charged at the Scottish basic rate (s. 227(4AA)), rather than at one marginal rate. Add `unused_pension_annual_allowance` for unused allowance carried forward from the three previous years (s. 228A; nil by default) and `pension_annual_allowance_chargeable_amount`. A trading loss no longer reduces relevant earnings from employment.
