- Split the LHA shared accommodation rate's non-dependant test by scheme. Universal Credit uses its own non-dependant and qualifying young person definitions (`universal_credit_renter_has_non_dependant`; UC Sch 4 paras 9 and 28). Housing Benefit uses reg 3 and the Child Benefit young person (`housing_benefit_claimant_has_non_dependant`; reg 13D(2)(a)(i)), including a non-dependant of joint occupiers. The rule treating every 16-year-old as a dependant is removed. The UC specified-renter test now uses UC responsibility only.
