The LHA shared accommodation rate's non-dependant tests use each scheme's own definitions within the benefit unit. Universal Credit's (`universal_credit_renter_has_non_dependant`; UC Regs 2013 Sch 4 paras 9 and 28) uses the UC non-dependant and qualifying young person instead of a blend of both schemes' young person tests, and the UC specified-renter test reads UC responsibility only (para 28(3)). Housing Benefit's (`housing_benefit_has_non_dependant`; HB Regs 2006 regs 3 and 13D(2)(a)(i)) uses the Child Benefit young person. Neither treats every 16-year-old as a dependant. Within the benefit unit, both tests now leave out the household head and a member who pays the household head as a boarder or lodger, as the non-dependant deductions already did (UC Sch 4 para 9(2)(d)-(e); HB reg 3(2)(d)-(e) and (4)), so such a member no longer stops a family getting the shared accommodation rate. The Housing Benefit severe disability premium's non-dependant test leaves out the household head and a member who pays them too (HB reg 3(2)(d)-(e) and (4); Sch 3 para 14). `universal_credit_renter_has_non_dependant` replaces `lha_renter_has_non_dependant`.
