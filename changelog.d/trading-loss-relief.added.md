Trade loss relief against general income (ITA 2007 s.64): a trading loss (`trading_loss`) now reduces net income for the loss-making year, within the s.24A cap of the greater of £50,000 and 25% of adjusted total income, taking non-savings income first, then savings, then dividends. New variables `trade_loss_relief_against_general_income`, `trade_loss_relief_against_savings_and_dividends`, `income_tax_relief_cap`, `adjusted_total_income` and `ni_class_4_profits`.
