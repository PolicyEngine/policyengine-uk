Trade loss relief against general income (ITA 2007 s.64): a trading loss (`trading_loss`) now reduces net income for the loss-making year, within the s.24A cap of the greater of £50,000 and 25% of adjusted total income (s.24A(8): total income less relieved pension contributions). Beyond non-savings income it comes off savings, then dividends, in the model's allowance order (#2106). New variables `net_income_before_trade_loss_relief`, `trade_loss_relief_against_general_income`, `trade_loss_relief_against_savings_and_dividends`, `income_tax_relief_cap`, `adjusted_total_income`, `ni_class_4_profits` and the input `ni_class_4_losses_brought_forward` (SSCBA 1992 Sch 2 para 3(4)(b)).
