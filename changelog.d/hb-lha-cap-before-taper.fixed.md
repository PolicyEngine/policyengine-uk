Apply the Local Housing Allowance cap to the eligible rent before the Housing Benefit taper, as SI 2006/213 regs 12D(2)(a), 70 and 71 (and SI 2006/214 regs 12D, 50 and 51) require. The taper is now deducted from the LHA rather than from the rent, so private tenants whose rent exceeds the LHA and whose income exceeds the applicable amount no longer receive the full LHA; previously the cap applied after the taper, which removed the taper whenever the rent exceeded the LHA by more than the taper.
