Apply the Local Housing Allowance cap to the eligible rent before the Housing Benefit taper, as SI 2006/213 regs 12D(2)(a), 70 and 71 (and SI 2006/214 regs 12D, 50 and 51; in Northern Ireland SR 2006/405 regs 13C, 68 and 69 and SR 2006/406 regs 13C, 48 and 49) require. The taper is now deducted from the LHA rather than from the rent. Previously the cap applied after the taper, which cut the taper by the excess of the rent over the LHA and removed it entirely when that excess was at least the taper, so private tenants with rent above the LHA and income above the applicable amount received too much.
