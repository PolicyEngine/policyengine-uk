Stop counting rent from property, and deducting tax on rent, interest and dividends, in the Income Support, Housing Benefit, Pension Credit and council tax reduction means tests, which treat income derived from capital as capital; count rent for letting part of the home less the £20 weekly sub-tenant disregard, except where a working-age council tax reduction applicant's income is the Universal Credit assessment. The residential finance-cost tax reduction now comes off the tax on property income. Council tax reduction now counts land and property other than the home as capital, at market value less 10% for the expenses of sale and less any debt secured on it; adds the national schemes' tariff income from capital (England for pensioners, Wales and Scotland); uses the Universal Credit assessment of capital for a working-age applicant with an award of Universal Credit, including one the benefit cap or deductions reduce to nil, where the scheme says so; and disregards the whole capital of an applicant on Income Support, income-based Jobseeker's Allowance or income-related Employment and Support Allowance in Wales, in Scotland before April 2022, and in the Kingston, Merton, Westminster and Oxford schemes.
