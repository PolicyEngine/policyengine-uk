Kept `parameters.baseline` unreformed when a `Scenario` applies parameter changes, so `baseline_vat` and the budget adjustments no longer compare a reform with itself. Structural reforms applied by a UK `Simulation` can now read the simulation they adjust, so a before-load `Scenario` change to DfE, DHSC or DfT spending constructs and scales each year's allocation without carrying an earlier year's change forward.
