- Add `partners_care_for_same_severely_disabled_person` (benefit unit). Two people caring for the same severely disabled person cannot both be entitled to Carer's Allowance (SSCBA s.70(7ZA)) or Carer Support Payment (SSI 2023/302 reg 5(3)), so they get one carer premium. Unless it is supplied, partners are treated as caring for the same person unless both are entitled with a reported Carer's Allowance or Carer Support Payment award (`carers_allowance_reported`), since caring hours cannot show who is cared for.
