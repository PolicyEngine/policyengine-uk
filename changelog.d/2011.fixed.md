The LHA size criteria now count other families' children, give foster children no bedroom of their own and add the foster-parent, adopter and overnight-care bedrooms (UC Regs 2013 Sch 4 paras 9-12 and 29(9A); HB Regs 2006 reg 13D(3)-(3B)), with new inputs for adoption placements, foster parents between placements, non-resident overnight carers and adopters who are a foster parent or close relative of the child (UC reg 89(3)(a)). Housing Benefit counts a child placed with a non-dependant, boarder or lodger in the claimant's size criteria (reg 21(3) names only the claimant or partner), and the legacy household, Income Support (Sch 1B paras 2 and 2A) and Tax-Free Childcare leave out a child placed for adoption.
