- Count the children of the household head's non-dependants (Universal Credit, UC Regs 2013 Sch 4 paras 9-10) and of non-dependants, boarders and lodgers (Housing Benefit, HB Regs 2006 reg 13D(12)) in the household head's LHA size criteria, sharing rooms with the head's own children. Give foster children no bedroom of their own, and foster parents, adopters with a child placed, and households with a non-resident overnight carer an additional bedroom (UC Sch 4 para 12; HB reg 13D(3A)-(3B)), with new inputs `is_placed_for_adoption`, `is_approved_foster_parent_without_placement` and `has_non_resident_overnight_carer`. Except foster parents from the shared accommodation rate (UC para 29(9A); HB "young individual"), and count armed forces independence payment in the UC para 29(5) exception. Give an occupier couple from another family one Housing Benefit bedroom (HB reg 13D(3)(a)). A child placed for adoption leaves the legacy household (HB reg 21(3), IS reg 16(4), JSA reg 78(4), ESA reg 156(5)) and is not a Tax-Free Childcare qualifying child (Childcare Payments (Eligibility) Regs 2015 reg 4(2)(d)). Treat a person under 18 looked after by a local authority and placed with the renter as the renter's foster child throughout the Universal Credit size criteria (`is_lha_foster_child`). Apply the 2017 extension of the overnight care bedroom beyond the renter or claimant from April 2017. Leave a child placed with the family out of the Income Support youngest-child test (IS reg 16(4)). Treat the looked-after flag as applying only under 18 (`is_looked_after_child`) in the UC, CTC, Pension Credit and legacy placement tests. Add Income Support Sch 1B paras 2 and 2A (a single claimant or lone parent with whom a child is placed by a local authority, or placed for adoption).
