- Count the children of the household head's non-dependants (Universal Credit, UC Regs 2013 Sch 4 paras 9-10) and of non-dependants, boarders and lodgers (Housing Benefit, HB Regs 2006 reg 13D(12)) in the household head's LHA size criteria, sharing rooms with the head's own children. Give foster children no bedroom of their own, and foster parents, adopters with a child placed, and households with a non-resident overnight carer an additional bedroom (UC Sch 4 para 12; HB reg 13D(3A)-(3B)), with new inputs `is_placed_for_adoption`, `is_approved_foster_parent_without_placement` and `has_non_resident_overnight_carer`. Except foster parents from the shared accommodation rate (UC para 29(9A); HB "young individual"), and count armed forces independence payment in the UC para 29(5) exception. Give an occupier couple from another family one Housing Benefit bedroom (HB reg 13D(3)(a)). A child placed for adoption leaves the legacy household (HB reg 21(3), IS reg 16(4), JSA reg 78(4), ESA reg 156(5)) and is not a Tax-Free Childcare qualifying child (Childcare Payments (Eligibility) Regs 2015 reg 4(2)(d)).
