Income-related ESA now ends when its abolition reaches the award: on 1 July 2026 in Great Britain (SI 2025/1148 art. 3A, inserted by SI 2026/409) and on 1 October 2026 in Northern Ireland (SR 2025/176 art. 3A, inserted by SR 2026/115). The new `gov.dwp.ESA.income.awards_payable` parameter is day-weighted across each fiscal year, so 2026-27 pays an award for 86/365 of the year in Great Britain and 178/365 in Northern Ireland, and 2027-28 onwards pays none. The new `esa_income_appointee_saving` input keeps an award that art. 3A(2) saves for an appointee; a Universal Credit claim still ends it (art. 3A(3)). Contributory ESA is unchanged.
