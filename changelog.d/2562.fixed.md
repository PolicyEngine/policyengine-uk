Model the 2026 income-related ESA abolition and its Universal Credit claims. An award pays to 30 June 2026 in Great Britain and 30 September 2026 in Northern Ireland (86/365 and 178/365 of 2026-27), and a family whose award the abolition reaches claims Universal Credit if it would claim when moved. From 2027-28 the award has ended and DWP has moved the family off its legacy benefits. A new input keeps an award saved for an appointee.
