Model income-related ESA payments up to the 2026 abolition dates and Universal Credit claims when a family's award starts to end. The model pays unsaved claims to 30 June 2026 in Great Britain and 30 September 2026 in Northern Ireland, using 86/365 and 178/365 of the 2026-27 year, and from 2027-28 moves each affected family off its legacy benefits. Users can preserve an award with the new appointee-saving input. A family in specified or temporary accommodation now keeps or can newly claim Housing Benefit for that rent beside a Universal Credit claim or DWP's move off legacy benefits, and Universal Credit leaves the rent out of its housing costs element; a hand-entered family with that status takes Housing Benefit up.
