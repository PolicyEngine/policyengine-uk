Apply the Universal Credit Act 2025 s. 1 uplifts to the Universal Credit standard allowance for 2027-28 to 2029-30 (3.1%, 4.0% and 4.8% above the CPI-only path from 2025-26). The allowance is now uprated by a dedicated index, benefit uprating CPI times one plus the uplift, so it grows by CPI times the change in uplift each year after the legislated 2026-27 rates, and by CPI alone after 2029-30. Switching the rebalancing reforms off, or changing a later year's uplift, now moves those years. `standard_allowance_uplift` was previously not read.
