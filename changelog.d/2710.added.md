Added the ISA annual subscription limit (£20,000) and the £12,000 cash ISA limit for people under 65 at the end of the tax year from 6 April 2027 (ISA Regulations 1998 reg 4ZA, as amended by SI 2026/1018), per-person cash and stocks-and-shares ISA subscription inputs, and `cash_isa_subscriptions_above_limit` and `isa_subscriptions_above_limit`, the subscriptions a limit leaves out. An opt-in response, `gov.contrib.behavioral_responses.cash_isa_excess`, off by default, sends cash above the cash limit to stocks-and-shares ISAs, taxable savings or other uses, and taxes the interest on the taxable part.
