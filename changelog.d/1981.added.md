- Added the Lifetime ISA holdings that microcosm datasets carry (`lifetime_isa_balance`, `has_lifetime_isa`, `household_lifetime_isa_balance`) and the Lifetime ISA withdrawal-charge parameters. Universal Credit, Housing Benefit, Income Support, income-based JSA, income-related ESA and Pension Credit now count a Lifetime ISA at its surrender value (75% of the balance under 60, the whole balance from 60) as capital of the holder's own benefit unit. Datasets without the columns are unaffected.
