- Stop the universal and working parent free hours in England, and Scotland's funded early learning and childcare, once a child has started school. A council meets both English duties for a child in a state-funded or council-funded reception class through the school place (DfE statutory guidance, footnotes 7 and 17, paras A4.8 and C.6), and a child in primary 1 is no longer an eligible pre-school child in Scotland (Children and Young People (Scotland) Act 2014 s.47(2)(b)). The new `early_years_child_in_school` is true when `current_education` is primary or a later stage, so datasets' 4-year-olds in reception no longer get funded hours; without a `current_education` input nothing changes. Set it to false for a fee-paying independent school's reception child, who can still take funded hours.
