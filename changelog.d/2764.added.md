Add `trading_allowance_claim_status`, a person input saying whether the input data's self-employment profit already deducts the trading allowance (claimant), deducts actual expenses above it (non-claimant), or is unknown (the default, which keeps the current treatment). A claimant's receipts are their profit plus `gov.simulation.trading_allowance_in_input_profits` (£1,000 from 2017-18, which reforms to the allowance do not move and which is not uprated), so a raise to any level relieves them by the allowance's excess over £1,000, up to their profit, and a cut below £1,000 leaves their profit as reported. A non-claimant gets no allowance at any level, on trade receipts or on income not otherwise charged. Income tax and Class 4 both read the result, so datasets without turnover can cost changes to the allowance, such as the Budget 2026 raise to £3,000, on the traders who already claim it.
