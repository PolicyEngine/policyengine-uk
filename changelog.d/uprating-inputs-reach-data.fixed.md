Dataset caching no longer couples a macro scenario to other simulations: a scenario built first left its projected incomes for its own baseline and every later simulation from the same `hf://` or `gs://` dataset, and one built after a baseline got current law's data. Parameter changes that change what the dataset projection reads, such as a reform dict on an OBR growth rate, are now written before the data is built, so incomes, rents and council tax follow them.
