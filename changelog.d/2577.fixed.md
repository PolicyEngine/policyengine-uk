Added overseas and other real estate as property components and counted each once in property wealth and in every means test's capital; a financial-only capital report now leaves them counted for Universal Credit and Pension Credit, and for Pension Credit also leaves owned land, other residential and non-residential property counted. Household land now applies regional land intensity to the residential, non-residential and other property components and adds direct UK land once at full value, so on a dataset whose saved property_wealth total differs from the sum of those components, household land moves by the regional intensity times that difference; on the certified file at data-year values, household land rises from about £5,113bn to £5,521bn (+£408bn, +8.0%).
