The model now counts overseas and other real estate in property wealth and in every means-tested capital assessment that includes household property. The Pension Credit formula now adds owned land, non-residential property and residential property other than the main residence to a financial-only capital report instead of letting the report replace them. The household land formula now applies regional land intensity to residential, non-residential and other real estate instead of property_wealth, and adds direct UK land once at full value. On the certified file, whose producer imputed the saved property_wealth total and the components separately, household land at data-year values rises from about £5,113bn to £5,521bn ([measurement](https://github.com/PolicyEngine/policyengine-uk/pull/2688#issuecomment-6449462413)).
