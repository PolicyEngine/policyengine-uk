Value share-like capital by asset type in every means test. Directly held shares, and any `corporate_wealth` a dataset does not itemise, take the 10% sale-expense deduction. Unit trusts count at full value (ADM H1673-H1674), as does the survey's combined unit and investment trust bucket, an approximation for its investment trusts. Stocks and shares ISAs count at their full withdrawal value, because ADM H1656 says nothing about costs of sale.
