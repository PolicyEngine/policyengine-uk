Value share-like capital by asset type in the six DWP capital assessments (Universal Credit, Pension Credit, Housing Benefit, Income Support, income-based JSA and income-related ESA). Directly held shares, and any `corporate_wealth` a dataset does not itemise, take the 10% sale-expense deduction. Unit trusts count at full value (ADM H1673-H1674), as does the survey's combined unit and investment trust bucket, an approximation for its investment trusts. Stocks and shares ISAs count at their full withdrawal value, a reading that rests on ADM H1656 saying nothing about costs of sale.
