- Give children and couples who cannot share a bedroom because of disability their own bedrooms in the LHA size criteria (UC Regs 2013 Sch 4 para 12(6), (6A), (8), (9)(c)-(d); HB Regs 2006 and HB (SPC) Regs 2006 reg 13D(3)(za), (zb), (ba)), with the new input `cannot_reasonably_share_bedroom_due_to_disability`. A child under 16 on the DLA care component at the middle or highest rate who cannot reasonably share is left out of the children's pairing and has a room of their own, so a room is added only where the child would otherwise share. A couple one of whom cannot share (on attendance allowance, armed forces independence payment, DLA care at the middle or highest rate or PIP daily living; attendance allowance only at the higher rate before 27 January 2025) has a bedroom each: for Universal Credit the renter's couple, for Housing Benefit any couple of occupiers. Housing Benefit allows these bedrooms only where the dwelling has them (`num_bedrooms`; assumed where not reported). In force from 4 December 2013 (children) and 1 April 2017 (couples).
