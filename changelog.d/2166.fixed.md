Modelled the temporary zero rate of VAT on domestic electricity in Great Britain from 1 October 2026 to 31 March 2027 (SI 2026/987), with Northern Ireland and gas staying at 5%. VAT on domestic electricity and gas is now charged on the households' own bills (`domestic_energy_vat`), at a new nation-split `gov.hmrc.vat.domestic_electricity_rate` and the reduced rate, instead of on a flat share of total consumption grossed up to receipts. Energy-bill VAT is not grossed up by the survey coverage or the household share of VAT receipts. The ONS domestic fuel and power share of spending now comes out of the reduced-rate share, which leaves at most 0.01% of spending in the generic reduced-rate base in any year since 2006.
