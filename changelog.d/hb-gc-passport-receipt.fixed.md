Key the Housing Benefit Guarantee Credit passport on a guarantee credit that is received (SI 2006/214 reg 26; NI SR 2006/406 reg 24), through a new `in_receipt_of_guarantee_credit` variable. Income and capital were disregarded whenever `guarantee_credit` was computed, including for pensioners who do not claim Pension Credit and for mixed-age couples, whom `is_pension_credit_eligible` pays no Pension Credit; those families now have their income and capital assessed, so capital over £16,000 ends their Housing Benefit. `is_pension_credit_eligible` omits the SI 2019/37 art 4 saving, so mixed-age couples it saves, who may be entitled to Pension Credit in law, lose the passport until that saving is modelled.
