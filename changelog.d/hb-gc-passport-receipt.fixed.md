Key the Housing Benefit Guarantee Credit passport on a guarantee credit that is received (SI 2006/214 reg 26; NI SR 2006/406 reg 24), through a new `in_receipt_of_guarantee_credit` variable. Income and capital were disregarded whenever `guarantee_credit` was computed, including for pensioners who do not claim Pension Credit and for mixed-age couples who cannot get it; those families now have their income and capital assessed, so capital over £16,000 ends their Housing Benefit.
