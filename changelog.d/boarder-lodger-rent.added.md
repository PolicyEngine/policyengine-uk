Model rent from boarders, lodgers and sub-tenants for the householder: rent-a-room relief in income tax, the £20 and board-and-lodging disregards in the legacy means tests (not Universal Credit), and no non-dependant deductions for a boarder or lodger and their partner, while a non-paying adult in their family stays a non-dependant. Under Universal Credit, a boarder's or lodger's child or qualifying young person gives the householder no bedroom.
