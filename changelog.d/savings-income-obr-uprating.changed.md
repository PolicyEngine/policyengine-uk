Dataset projection now grows savings interest, other investment income, interest on cash held in non-cash ISAs and, in datasets on the additive ISA contract, ISA interest by the OBR's savings income forecast per person from 2022-23 to 2025-26 (October 2026 EFO, Table 4.5), and by GDP per head in other years as before. Datasets on the legacy gross contract grow gross interest and hold its ISA part flat, as before. 2023-24 savings income therefore reaches 2025-26 on the OBR's path (35.8%) rather than GDP per head and population (10.3%).
