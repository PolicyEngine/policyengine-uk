Fix `calculate_dependency_contributions` where the target, or a float variable it reads directly, holds non-zero months: restoring them over the year raised "Inconsistent input", and a monthly target kept those months at zero. The function now recalculates the target on a throwaway branch, takes its reads from the calculation that gave the values the simulation holds, and leaves that simulation as it found it, apart from its own calculation of the target. A monthly target now depends on what its months read, not on itself. The function now gives an empty result a float dtype, so `top_n`, `create_waterfall_chart` and `calculate_dependency_contribution_change` accept a target with no contributions. The function's docstring lists what it raises for and its limitations.
