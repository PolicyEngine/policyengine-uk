Fix `calculate_dependency_contributions` for monthly variables (Class 1 NI, child benefit amounts, fuel duty, energy price guarantee). When the target or a variable it reads directly is defined monthly and its months hold non-zero values, restoring the value over the year raised "Inconsistent input", and for a monthly target it left the simulation with those months at zero. Each dependency is now zeroed on a throwaway branch, so the simulation passed in is never changed, and a monthly target's dependencies are what its months read rather than the target itself.
