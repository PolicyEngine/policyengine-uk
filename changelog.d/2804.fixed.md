Excluded from the Universal Credit qualifying young person test a person who reports their own income-related ESA, income-based JSA, Income Support, Housing Benefit, Child Tax Credit or Working Tax Credit while the model pays it on that report (its year gate and the model's legacy closure) and no reform neutralises it, and a claimant or partner on the couple's tax credit award (SI 2014/1230 reg. 28). The formula for Scotland's working-age Council Tax Reduction reads the Universal Credit Regulations 2013 reg. 5 test without reg. 28.
