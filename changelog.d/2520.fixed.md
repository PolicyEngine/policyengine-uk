Assess a working-age Oxford Council Tax Reduction applicant who receives Universal Credit on the Appendix 3 income Oxford City Council sets from April 2026: DWP's net earned income, from which DWP deducts pension contributions in full, plus the award before deductions for debts and other unearned income, less £48.15 a week of each carer element and £84.46 a week of each disabled child element. Other income includes Child Benefit, child maintenance, the Scottish Carer Supplement and the other regular receipts the council does not ignore in Appendix 3, but not interest, dividends, rent, Universal Credit's assumed yield from capital or the miscellaneous income the model already counts in DWP's net earned income. The model had assessed these applicants on the income for applicants without Universal Credit, on which the council deducts only half of pension contributions and leaves out Child Benefit and child maintenance. Some of their reductions rise and some fall, a reform of Oxford's pension contribution share no longer moves them, and model years before 2026 keep their earlier results.
