Assess a working-age Oxford Council Tax Reduction applicant who receives Universal Credit on the scheme's Appendix 3 income from April 2026: DWP's net earned income, which deducts pension contributions in full, plus the award before deductions for debts and other unearned income, less £48.15 a week of each carer element and £84.46 a week of each disabled child element. Other income counts Child Benefit, child maintenance and the other receipts Appendix 3 does not ignore, but not interest, dividends, rent or Universal Credit's assumed yield from capital. The model had assessed these applicants on the income for applicants without Universal Credit, which deducts half of pension contributions and leaves out Child Benefit and child maintenance. Some of their reductions rise and some fall, a reform of Oxford's pension contribution share no longer moves them, and model years before 2026 keep their earlier results.
