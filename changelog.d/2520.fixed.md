Assess a working-age Oxford Council Tax Reduction applicant who receives Universal Credit on the scheme's Appendix 3 income: DWP's net earned income, which deducts pension contributions in full, plus the award before deductions for debts and other income, less £48.15 a week of each carer element and £84.46 a week of each disabled child element. The model had assessed them on the income for applicants without Universal Credit, which deducts half of pension contributions. Some of these applicants' reductions rise and some fall, and a reform of Oxford's pension contribution share no longer moves them.
