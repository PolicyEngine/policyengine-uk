Assess a working-age Oxford Council Tax Reduction applicant who receives Universal Credit on the scheme's Appendix 3 income: DWP's net earned income, which deducts pension contributions in full, plus the award and other income, less £48.15 a week of each carer element and £84.46 a week of each disabled child element. The model had assessed them on the income for applicants without Universal Credit, which deducts half of pension contributions, so a reform of Oxford's pension contribution share moved their reductions.
