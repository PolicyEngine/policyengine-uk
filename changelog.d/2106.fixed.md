The model now deducts the Personal Allowance, other allowances and trade loss relief from non-savings, savings and dividend income so that the taxpayer pays the least tax (ITA 2007 s. 25(2)). From 2027-28 it takes them off other income before property, savings and dividend income (s. 25(3A)). It previously always deducted them from non-savings income first, then savings, then dividends.
