The model now sets the Personal Allowance, other allowances and trade loss relief against non-savings, savings and dividend income in the way that gives the least tax (ITA 2007 s. 25(2)), and from 2027-28 against other income before property, savings and dividend income (s. 25(3A)), where it previously always took them from non-savings income first. Pension relief stays on non-savings income, and the income tax that Universal Credit deducts from earnings still follows the order non-savings, savings, dividends.
